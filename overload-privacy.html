Privacy Policy

Overload Workout Log · Effective October 18, 2026

Overload Workout Log ("Overload", "the app", "we") is a workout logging app published by Dolair. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

The short version

  • You can use Overload without an account. Without one, your workouts and exercises stay on your device.
  • If you create an optional account, we store your email address and your synced workouts and exercises so they follow you across devices.
  • Overload has no ads and no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Overload is for adults and is not directed to children.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Information we collect

Without an account. Your workouts (dates, names, durations, exercises, sets, weights, and reps), your custom exercises, your onboarding choices, and your Pro status are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.

With an account (optional). If you sign up, we collect:

  • Your email address and password. The password is handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form. We cannot read it.
  • A user ID created for your account.
  • Synced app data: your workouts, your exercise list, and a last-updated time.

Subscriptions. Overload Pro is sold through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). Google or Apple handles payment, and we never see or store your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play or Apple to confirm or restore your purchase, and keeps your Pro status on your device.

Connection data. When the app opens or syncs, it connects to our backend and loads a software library (the open-source Supabase client) from public content delivery networks. Like any online service, our backend provider and those networks can see your IP address and basic request details such as device type when the app connects. We do not use this to identify you or to track you across other apps or websites.

What we do not collect. The Android app asks only for internet access, network status, and Google Play Billing. Overload does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos, does not read health or fitness data from other apps, and does not collect advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To sync your workouts and exercises across your devices.
  • To send account emails you request or need, such as email confirmation and password reset links.
  • To confirm or restore your subscription and unlock Pro features.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Supabase, our backend provider, which hosts authentication, the database for synced data, and account emails.
  • Google Play (Android) and Apple (iOS), which process subscriptions.
  • Public content delivery networks (esm.sh, jsDelivr, Skypack) that serve the Supabase client library, as described above.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Our providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Security

Data sent between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, go to the Account tab, sign in, tap "Delete my account", then tap "Yes, delete everything". Your account and synced data are erased from our backend. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account and data. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play or the App Store.

Your choices and rights

You can use Overload without an account, sign out at any time, and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, or California), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Overload is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
