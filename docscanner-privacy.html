Privacy Policy

Document Scanner: OCR, PDF · Effective October 18, 2026

Document Scanner: OCR, PDF ("the app", "we") is a document scanning app for Android, published by Dolair. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends off your device, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your scans, recognized text and PDFs stay on your device. The app has no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your documents.
  • Scanning uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, and text recognition uses ML Kit's on-device text recognizer. Both work on your device.
  • The app has no ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs of ours, and no tracking. We do not sell personal information.
  • The optional Pro subscription is sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Google components used by the app (Google Play services, ML Kit and Google Play Billing) may collect limited device and diagnostic information, described below.
  • You can delete a document, or all documents, at any time in the app.

What the app stores on your device

When you scan or import a document, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • The page images (JPEG files) produced by the scanner or imported from your photos or files.
  • The text recognized from those pages, and anything you type or edit in that text.
  • The document title, category and similar details you choose.
  • Your app settings (such as theme and onboarding choices) and your Pro status.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. The app opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your documents are not copied to your Google account by that feature.

Scanning and text recognition

Scanning is handled by the Google ML Kit Document Scanner, which is delivered through Google Play services. Google states that the entire document scanner flow operates on-device. Because the scanner uses the camera access that Google Play services already holds, the app itself does not request the camera permission. The scanner returns the finished page images to the app.

Text recognition (OCR) uses the ML Kit text recognition library that is bundled inside the app and runs on your device. It reads printed text in Latin-script languages (for example English, Spanish, French and German). It can make mistakes, so check anything important.

Importing, exporting and sharing

  • Import. When you choose photos or files to import, the app copies the images you select into its private storage.
  • Export. When you tap Share or Save, the app creates a PDF, text file or set of images and passes it to the app or folder you choose. What happens to it after that is governed by that app or service, not by us.
  • Copy text. If you copy recognized text, it is placed on your device clipboard, which other apps may be able to read.

Temporary export files are kept in the app's cache and cleaned up by the app.

Information collected by Google components

The app uses components provided by Google. As described in Google's documentation, these may send limited technical data to Google, encrypted in transit using HTTPS:

  • Google Play services and ML Kit: device information (such as manufacturer, model and operating system version), app information (package name and version), per-installation identifiers that are not intended to uniquely identify you or your device, and performance and diagnostic data (such as latency and error codes). This data is not your documents or recognized text.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy or restore a subscription, Google Play processes the purchase and gives the app your purchase status. Payment is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and we never see or store your payment details.

We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or microphone data, and it has no account system. It requests internet access, network status and Google Play Billing, which are needed for subscriptions and for Google's components to work.

Security

Your documents are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. Use a screen lock to protect your device. Data that Google components send over the internet is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Documents stay on your device until you delete them. To delete one, open it and choose Delete. To delete everything, open Settings and choose "Delete all documents". Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored in it. Files you exported or shared to other apps or folders are outside the app and must be deleted there. Because we do not hold your documents on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your choices and rights

You control your documents entirely: you can create, export and delete them at any time, and you can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Menu, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions). Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights over personal information held about you. Since we hold none of your documents, such requests mostly concern your contact with us; email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
