Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 • God's Wonderful Alphabet: Christian ABCs

God's Wonderful Alphabet is designed with love for children and families. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our youngest learners. Our app strictly adheres to the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Guidelines for the Kids Category.

1. Zero Data Collection on Children

We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Specifically:

  • No children's names, ages, birthdays, or photos are ever collected.
  • No location data or GPS coordinates are accessed.
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers (IDFA) are tracked.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are present in the app.

2. 100% Ad-Free Experience

God's Wonderful Alphabet is completely ad-free. We do not display third-party banner advertisements, interstitial ads, video rewards, or behavioral targeted advertising of any kind.

3. Parental Gate & Family Accounts

Adult parents or guardians may optionally set up a family account to manage multi-device sync or subscriptions. All settings, account modifications, and external communication links are protected behind a Parental Gate to ensure children cannot make accidental purchases or leave the app.

4. Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

All subscription payments (Monthly Family Pass at $4.99/mo with 3-Day Free Trial, or Platinum Lifetime Pass at $19.99) are processed exclusively by Apple through Apple StoreKit 2. We never see, process, or store your credit card or billing details.
