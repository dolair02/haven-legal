Privacy Policy

NovaMath K-5 (Android) · Effective October 30, 2026

NovaMath K-5 ("NovaMath", "the app", "we") is a math practice app for children in Kindergarten through Grade 5 (ages 4 to 11), their parents, and their teachers. It is published by Dolair. This policy explains what the Android app stores, what we collect (very little), and the choices you have.

The short version

  • NovaMath has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect personal information from children or from anyone else.
  • Your learner's first name or nickname, grade, companion pet, stars, and skill progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • NovaMath has no ads and no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • The app has no chat, no social features, and no way for children to share anything with other people.
  • Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.
  • You can remove everything the app stores at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

What the app stores on your device

To remember where your learner left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device: the first name or nickname you type during setup, the selected grade, the chosen companion pet and any unlocked companions, stars and streak counts, the daily goal, per-skill math progress, sound and read-aloud settings, and a flag that records your subscription status. This information does not leave your device and is not sent to us or to any third party by the app. Android or Google may back up app data to your Google account if your device backup is turned on; that is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Read-aloud voice

Questions can be read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine and a few short audio clips bundled with the app. Which engine speaks, and whether it works offline, depends on your device's settings and its text-to-speech provider.

Subscriptions and Google Play

NovaMath is offered as an auto-renewing subscription with a free trial for eligible new subscribers. Purchases are processed by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles your payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your subscription is active so it can unlock the lessons, and keeps the result on your device. Google may collect information as described in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Google Play also offers parental controls, such as requiring approval for purchases, in its Family Link and Play Store settings.

Permissions and SDKs

The Android app asks only for internet access and Google Play Billing. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, photos, or storage permissions, and it does not collect advertising identifiers. The only third-party code that handles user-related information is Google's Play Billing Library. The app contains no advertising, analytics, social, or login SDKs.

Children's privacy

NovaMath is designed for children, and we built it so that we do not need to collect their personal information. There are no accounts, no user profiles stored on our servers, no chat, no user-generated content, no location features, and no behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app keeps its data on the device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

The app may link to Google Play pages (for example, to manage a subscription) or to this policy. Those open in Google Play or your browser and are governed by their own policies.

Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Any connection the app makes to Google Play is protected by Google using encryption in transit. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data, so there is no account to delete. Data saved on your device stays there until you remove it. To erase it, open Android Settings, then Apps, then NovaMath K-5, then Storage, and tap "Clear storage" (or uninstall the app). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without giving us any information, change or remove the learner's name by clearing the app's storage, and cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions). Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. Because we do not collect it, we will usually have nothing to provide, but email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app or in the Google Play listing where required.
