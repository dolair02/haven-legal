Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026 • Effective Date: September 26, 2026
Overview: Handoff ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy while providing a secure, flake-proof environment for in-person local peer-to-peer exchanges. This Privacy Policy details how your information is collected, encrypted, processed, and safeguarded when using the Handoff: Safe Local Trade mobile app and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure in-person transactions and prevent marketplace scams and ghosting, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Name, verified email address, phone number, and account authentication tokens.
  • Geolocation Data (Location Services): Real-time background and foreground GPS location when an in-person exchange is active to power the 50ft Safe Spot proximity radar, geofenced arrival confirmations, and anti-ghosting meeting verification. Geolocation data is never sold or used for targeted third-party advertising.
  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly when you take photos of items to be traded, scan serial numbers/IMEIs for FBI NCIC & GSMA verification, or inspect paper banknotes using optical ultraviolet simulation. Photos are processed on-device whenever possible.
  • Transaction & Escrow Data: Records of scheduled handoffs, $5 anti-flake escrow deposits, Safe Spot check-in timestamps, and dispute resolutions.
  • Device & Biometric Authentication: Local Face ID / Touch ID validation status (biometric templates never leave your secure enclave on the device) used to authenticate instant cashouts and funds releases.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to maintain safety, prevent fraud, and facilitate successful local trades:

  • Enforce the Anti-Flake $5 Trust Escrow policy and issue instant penalty payouts if a party ghosts without notice.
  • Provide verified 24/7 CCTV Safe Spot navigation, police station bay meetups, and partner merchant check-ins.
  • Detect counterfeit currency and query national databases for reported stolen serial numbers and blacklisted mobile phones.
  • Provide customer support, resolve meetup disputes, and facilitate instant debit card cashouts via payment partners.

3. Subscriptions & Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Handoff offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Handoff Premium Monthly at $5.99/month and Handoff Premium Annual at $49.99/year), each including a 3-day introductory free trial.

  • All subscription billing is handled directly and securely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit 2 infrastructure.
  • We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or Apple Pay billing information on our servers.
  • Transaction receipts are cryptographically verified using Apple App Store Server APIs to maintain your active entitlement state across your Apple devices.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is shared exclusively under the following strict circumstances:

  • Your Trading Counterpart: Only minimal information needed for the meetup (first name, profile verification status, and en-route proximity status) is displayed to the verified buyer/seller during an active deal.
  • Law Enforcement & Safety: In the event of a verified crime, robbery, or violence during a meetup, trade records and check-in telemetry may be provided to law enforcement authorities in compliance with valid legal processes.
  • Payment Gateways: Escrow holds and instant cashouts are processed via PCI-DSS compliant financial partners.

5. Data Security & Retention

All network communications between the Handoff app and our servers use TLS 1.3 encryption. User identification data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Meeting GPS telemetry is automatically purged after the transaction is successfully completed or closed.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to inspect, update, or permanently delete your account and associated transaction history at any time directly in the app settings or by submitting a written request to support@handoff.trade. Upon receiving your deletion request, your personal identifiers will be permanently scrubbed within 30 days.
