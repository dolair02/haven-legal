Privacy Policy

Haven: Private Wealth & Risk • Effective Date: September 22, 2026
Zero Cloud Tracking • 100% On-Device Vault

Our Core Privacy Commitment: Haven was built specifically for individuals who refuse to let third parties, cloud trackers, or data brokers profit off their private financial data. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your financial transactions, portfolio balances, or personal identification. Everything executes entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional financial apps that monetize user transaction histories or sell data to advertisers, Haven operates on a Zero-Knowledge, Client-Side Architecture:

  • No Bank Account or Brokerage Credentials: Haven never asks for or stores your bank passwords, API tokens, or OAuth credentials.
  • No Transaction History Uploads: Bank statements, CSV imports, and TSV files processed in the Statement Cleaner are parsed strictly inside your device's memory.
  • No Portfolio Balances: Your holdings, ticker quantities, buy prices, and dividend records never touch an external server.
  • No Advertising Identifiers (IDFA): We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No Location or Biometric Data: Face ID and Touch ID authentication are handled securely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on your hardware Secure Enclave. We never receive biometric templates.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you enter into Haven is stored exclusively within your device's local application storage and encrypted sandbox:

  • Military-Grade Encryption: When vault protection is enabled, your data is secured using 256-bit AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation.
  • Self-Contained Exports: You have 100% data portability. You can export your full data anytime to an encrypted .haven vault file, CSV spreadsheet, Word document (.doc), or PDF report.
  • Instant Purge: You can permanently wipe all data from your device at any time by selecting "Delete All Data" in the Settings menu.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Haven offers an optional auto-renewable subscription ($4.99/month or $49.99/year with a 3-day free trial). All transactions and payment processing are handled directly by Apple Media Services via the App Store.

  • We do not collect or process your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
  • All subscriptions are subject to Apple's Standard Terms of Service and Apple Privacy Policy.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

4. Network Communications & Market Quotes

Haven is designed to function fully offline in airplane mode. If you enable optional live market quotes or dividend schedules, public ticker price updates are fetched directly via public API endpoints. These queries contain only standard public ticker symbols (e.g., AAPL, VOO) and contain zero user identifiers, portfolio balances, or account metadata.

5. Children's Privacy

Haven is a general audience finance utility and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, Haven complies with COPPA and global child safety standards.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & GDPR Rights

Under regulations such as GDPR and CCPA, users have rights regarding data access, deletion, and portability:

  • Right to Know / Access: All your data is visible to you immediately inside the application at all times.
  • Right to Deletion: You can wipe all local storage with one tap in Settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data under any circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: 1-click export to CSV, PDF, or encrypted JSON file.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any future modifications to this Privacy Policy will be published at this URL with an updated Effective Date. Since our architecture does not transmit data, our core promise of zero-cloud tracking remains immutable.
