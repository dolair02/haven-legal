Privacy Policy

TIL: Curious Facts

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

TIL: Curious Facts is designed with privacy as our core priority. We believe educational and curiosity apps should never spy on their users, sell their attention, or harvest personal data.

1. Data Collection & Sovereignty

TIL: Curious Facts operates locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, enter your email address, or provide any personal information to use the app.

  • Favorites & Saved Facts: Saved locally on your device storage (and optionally synced via your private Apple iCloud account if enabled).
  • Reading History: Stored locally on your device and never uploaded to remote analytics servers.
  • Audio Narration: Generated and streamed without personal identifiers.

2. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Trackers

TIL: Curious Facts contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero data brokers, and zero behavioral trackers (such as Facebook Pixel or Google Analytics). You will never see third-party ads within this app.

3. In-App Purchases & Apple StoreKit

All subscription purchases (Curious Facts Club Weekly, Monthly, and Annual) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never see or store your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials. All transactions are securely governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at dolair02@gmail.com.
