Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 • Talking Macros

Your privacy is fundamental to how we build our products. Talking Macros is designed to minimize data collection, process your nutrition information privately, and safeguard your personal health metrics.

1. Information We Process

  • Nutrition & Meal Records: Spoken food logs, ingredients, calorie counts, and macronutrient targets (protein, carbs, fats) you enter into the app are stored locally on your device and inside Apple Health.
  • Microphone & Speech Dictation: When you use voice logging, audio input is captured temporarily via Apple's native Speech Recognition framework solely to transcribe your meal into text. We do not store, sell, or listen to raw audio recordings.
  • Camera Usage: When you scan barcodes or take a photo of a food plate, the camera is used strictly in real-time to detect UPC codes and food types. We do not access your photo library without explicit action, and photos are not harvested for advertising.

2. Apple HealthKit Compliance (Guideline 5.1.3)

Apple HealthKit Guarantee: Talking Macros integrates with Apple HealthKit (HKHealthStore) to write dietary calories and macronutrients. In strict compliance with Apple's developer guidelines, your HealthKit data is never sold, shared with advertising platforms, data brokers, or used for data mining.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Data Selling

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information, dietary habits, or health data to advertisers, analytics brokers, or marketing networks. Talking Macros is 100% ad-free.

4. Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

All subscription billing ($3.99/month, $29.99/year) and free trial management are handled securely through Apple's StoreKit 2 infrastructure. Talking Macros never sees, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your meal and macro logs are stored on your device and in your personal Apple Health repository, you maintain complete control. Deleting the app or clearing your Apple Health logs immediately purges the data.
