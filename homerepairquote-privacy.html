Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 • Home Repair Quote

Your privacy is paramount. Home Repair Quote is engineered with strict privacy protections so homeowners can review contractor quotes and receive competitive bids without fear of unwanted sales calls, aggressive lead brokers, or invasive tracking.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: If you create an account, we store your email address solely for account access and authentication.
  • Estimate Documents & Photos: When you upload a contractor invoice or estimate for AI decryption, it is processed securely to perform line-item analysis and is never sold or used for targeted third-party advertising.
  • Camera Access: Camera access is requested strictly when you choose to photograph an estimate or job site.

2. Zero-Spam Guarantee & Blind Bidding

Unlike traditional contractor lead marketplaces that sell homeowner phone numbers to numerous competing contractors, Home Repair Quote uses anonymous Ghost IDs. Contractors can never view your phone number, email, or physical street address unless you explicitly choose to share it.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to contractors, lead aggregators, or advertisers.

4. Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

All subscription payments ($7.99/month, $69.99/year) and free trial entitlements are securely handled through Apple's StoreKit 2 infrastructure. We do not process or store credit card numbers or financial details.
