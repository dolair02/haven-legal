Frequently Asked Questions

How does voice food logging work?
Tap the microphone button on the main dashboard and speak your meal naturally (e.g. "3 scrambled eggs with sourdough toast and 8 oz skim milk"). Talking Macros uses native on-device speech processing to identify the foods, calculate total calories, protein, carbs, and fats, and update your daily rings in seconds.
How does Talking Macros sync with Apple HealthKit?
Talking Macros integrates directly with Apple HealthKit. When you grant permissions, meals logged in Talking Macros automatically write dietary energy (calories), protein, carbohydrates, and fats to Apple Health. Your data is stored securely on your device and inside Apple Health; we never share HealthKit data with third parties or advertisers.
How does the 3-day free trial and subscription work?
Talking Macros offers a 3-day free trial providing unrestricted access to all features (voice recognition, photo scanning, verified barcode scanner, HealthKit sync). After the 3 days, you can choose to continue with either the Monthly Plan ($3.99/mo) or Annual Plan ($29.99/yr). You can cancel at any time during the trial at no charge.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
All subscriptions are managed securely through your Apple ID:
1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Talking Macros to switch plans or cancel auto-renewal.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open Talking Macros, tap the Settings icon on the top right of the dashboard, and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple StoreKit 2 will verify your active subscription and unlock your Pro access instantly.
