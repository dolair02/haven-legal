What the app is

PawRx is a tool for keeping your own records about your pets: medicines and dose schedules, reminders, vaccines, vet visits, weights, notes, costs, and printable summaries. Everything it shows is based on what you enter, plus general information built into the app. It is for general informational use only. It is not veterinary advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not replace a veterinarian.

Always follow your veterinarian's instructions, and check any dose or schedule with your vet or pharmacist. The built-in guidance, including the list of foods and substances that can harm pets, is general, may be incomplete or out of date, and is not a diagnosis. If you think your pet is sick, hurt or has eaten something harmful, contact your vet, an emergency animal clinic or a pet poison helpline straight away; do not wait for the app. Poison helplines and clinics are independent of us and may charge a fee.

Reminders

The app schedules reminders on your phone. Android, your phone's maker, battery saver, Do Not Disturb and notification settings can delay, silence or block them, and a reminder may arrive a few minutes late. Do not rely on reminders alone for medicine that cannot be missed; check your schedule as well.
