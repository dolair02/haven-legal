Privacy Policy

Macros (Android) · Effective October 20, 2026

Macros ("the app", "we") helps you track calories, protein, carbs, fat, water, workouts and weight, and works out a daily plan from the details you give it. It is published by Dolair. This policy covers the Android app and explains what it stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have. Macros for iPhone is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. Your plan, food log, workouts, water, weight and progress photos stay on your phone. We run no database that receives them.
  • Only two features send anything off your phone, and only when you use them: AI analysis (a meal photo, a Nutrition Facts label photo, or a meal description you type) and barcode lookup (the digits you type).
  • For AI analysis, your photo or text is sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI, which returns an estimate of the nutrition. Photos are shrunk and stripped of location data first. Our server does not keep your photos or text. Google's own terms apply once it receives them (see below).
  • The app has no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools of ours. We do not sell personal information.
  • Macros Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can erase everything the app holds by clearing its storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What the app stores on your device

As you use the app, it saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your profile and plan: date of birth, height, current and goal weight, your goal (such as losing or gaining weight) and weekly pace, diet type, your choices about adding burned calories back and rolling calories over, your answers to the setup questions, and an optional name.
  • Your calorie, protein, carb, fat and water targets, and your BMI, which the app works out from your details.
  • Your food log: meals and food items with portions, calories, protein, carbs and fat, and any quick-add foods you save.
  • Workouts and the calories they burned, water you log, and weight entries.
  • Progress photos you take. The app keeps a smaller copy of each.
  • Groups you join or create. These exist only on your phone. There is no online community in the Android app.
  • Your settings and your subscription status, which the app reads from Google Play.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Your weight, height, birth date and eating records are personal health information, so please protect your phone with a screen lock.

What leaves your phone, and when

AI analysis of meals, labels and descriptions. When you use "Scan Meal Photo", "Snap Nutrition Facts Label Photo" or "Describe Your Meal" and tap the button to analyze, the app sends the photo or the text you typed (plus the extra note you add, if any) and fixed instructions to a small relay that we run, hosted by Supabase. The relay passes the request on to Google's Gemini API and returns the estimate to your phone. Before a photo is sent, the app redraws it as a smaller JPEG, which removes the location and other camera data stored inside the picture. The app does not send your name, birth date, weight or any of your other records with the request. Our relay does not save or log your photos or text. Like any web server, the relay and its host see technical details of a request, such as your IP address, the time and the size of the request, and the relay uses your IP address briefly to limit abuse.

Google processes what the relay sends it under the Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy. Google may keep logs of requests for a limited time and, depending on the kind of Gemini API service in use, may use submitted content to improve its products and services, which can include review by people. We cannot delete anything that Google holds. Please do not photograph or type anything you would not want Google to process, such as other people's faces, ID cards, or documents with personal details. AI results are estimates and can be wrong; check them before you rely on them.

Barcode lookup. When you type a barcode and tap Lookup, the app sends those digits straight to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to get the product's nutrition. Open Food Facts receives the barcode and your IP address and handles them under its own privacy policy. The lookup does not go through our server.

Google Play. The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to offer the optional Macros Pro subscription. When you start, restore or manage a subscription, the app talks to Google Play, which processes the purchase and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details. When the app's Google Play components communicate with Google, Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and device or other identifiers, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it and we do not use it for advertising.

Export Summary Report. On the Profile screen this shows a short text with your calorie and macro goals, current weight and BMI and, where your phone allows it, offers Android's share sheet. Anything you share goes only to the app you choose.

Nothing else leaves your phone. The app has no account system, no analytics and no advertising network. Links such as Manage subscription, Privacy Policy and Terms open in your browser or in Google Play.

Photos and the camera

When you choose to take a photo (a meal, a nutrition label or a progress photo), Android asks your permission to use the camera the first time, and the app opens your phone's camera. The app does not browse your photo library. Android's camera may leave the original, full-size picture in the app's private folder on your phone, and that picture may carry location data if your camera adds it. It stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Only the smaller copy that the app creates is ever sent for AI analysis, and progress photos never leave your phone.

Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions: internet access and network status (to reach the AI relay, Open Food Facts and Google Play), the Google Play Billing permission (to offer the subscription), and the camera (only when you choose to take a photo). The app does not ask to use your microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone or photo library.

Security

The data you enter is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. Everything the app sends over the internet is encrypted in transit. The AI relay only accepts requests that carry a key built into the app, and it limits how many requests one internet address can make. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Android's automatic backup is allowed for this app. If your phone's Google backup is turned on, Android may copy some or all of the data the app keeps on your phone, including your food log and progress photos, to your Google Account's backup and restore it on a new phone. We do not receive or control those backups; you manage them in your phone's settings (Settings, then Google, then Backup). The app has no backup file of its own. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage or lose your phone without a backup, the records are gone, and we cannot recover them.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete a food entry or a workout one at a time in the app. To erase everything at once, including weight entries and progress photos, clear the app's storage (Settings, then Apps, then Macros, then Storage, then Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Because we do not keep your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; the relay does not keep your photos or text. Content that Google received for AI analysis, and barcodes that Open Food Facts received, are held under those services' own policies and we cannot delete them.

Your choices and rights

You decide what to enter and whether to use the AI features or barcode lookup; the rest of the app works on your phone without sending your records anywhere. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Menu, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions), or from the Profile screen in the app, which opens Google Play for you. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights over personal information held about you. Since we hold none of the data you enter, such requests mostly concern your contact with us; email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Macros is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app's AI features, email us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
