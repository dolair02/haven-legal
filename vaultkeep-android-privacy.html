Privacy Policy

VaultKeep (Android) · Effective October 25, 2026

VaultKeep ("the app", "we") is a home inventory and warranty tracker: you list your belongings with photos, serial numbers, values and warranty details, and keep track of maintenance. It is published by Dolair. This policy covers the Android app and explains what it stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your items, photos, receipts, serial numbers, notes and values are stored on your phone only. The app has no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your data.
  • The app has no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools of ours. We do not sell personal information.
  • AI scanning is optional and off until you add your own Google Gemini API key. When you scan a photo, the app sends that photo from your phone straight to Google using your key. We never receive it.
  • VaultKeep Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can delete any item, wipe everything from Settings, or uninstall the app at any time.

What the app stores on your device

As you use the app, it saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Items you add: name, brand, model and serial numbers, category, room, storage container, purchase date, price, store, warranty details, condition, value and notes.
  • Photos you attach to an item, such as a photo of the item, a receipt or a serial plate. You choose each photo with Android's own photo or file picker, and the app keeps a resized copy inside its private storage. The original stays where it was.
  • Maintenance schedules you create, and optional insurance details you type in (carrier, policy number, coverage limit, deductible).
  • Your settings, your optional PIN lock, your optional Gemini API key, and your Pro plan status.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Android's backup feature, if it is turned on for your device, may include this app's data in your device backup in your Google account. That backup is made and controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it.

Optional AI scanning (Google Gemini)

VaultKeep does not include AI of its own. If you add a Google Gemini API key in Settings, the AI Capture and AI Room Scan features become available. Each time you use one of them, the app sends the photo you picked, together with a short instruction, from your phone directly to Google's Gemini API using your key, and shows you what Google returns so you can check it before saving. We do not receive the photo or the result. Photos can contain personal information, such as an address on a receipt or a serial number, so please only scan photos you are comfortable sending to Google.

What Google does with the photo is covered by the Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy, and by the settings of the Google account that owns your key. If you do not add a key, nothing is sent and the rest of the app works with items you enter by hand.

Subscriptions and Google Play

The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to offer the optional VaultKeep Pro subscription. When you start, restore or manage a subscription, the app talks to Google Play, which processes the purchase and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details.

When the app's Google Play components communicate with Google, Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and device or other identifiers, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This is not your inventory data. We do not receive it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

The app asks for internet access and network status so that Google Play Billing can reach Google Play, so that optional AI scanning can reach Google, and so links open, and for the Google Play Billing permission. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar or storage permissions.

Links to other websites

Some screens offer links, for example to search for a product manual. They open in your browser, and the privacy policy of that website applies.

Security

The data you enter is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. The optional PIN lock is a screen lock inside the app; it keeps casual viewers out but does not encrypt your data, so also use your phone's own screen lock. Communication with Google Play and with Google Gemini is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. To delete an item, open it and choose Remove Item. To remove everything, open Settings and choose Wipe All Data. If you forget your PIN, the lock screen offers a "Forgot your PIN?" option that erases everything stored in the app so you can start again. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, also removes everything the app stores on your phone. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your choices and rights

You control your records entirely. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions) or with Manage in Google Play inside the app, and you can remove your Gemini API key in Settings at any time. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights over personal information held about you. Since we hold none of your inventory data, such requests mostly concern your contact with us; email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
