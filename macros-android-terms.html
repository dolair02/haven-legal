Do not ignore professional advice, or delay getting it, because of something in the app. If tracking food or weight makes you feel unwell or anxious, stop using the app and speak to a health professional.

Estimates and AI

Calories, macros, BMI and daily targets are estimates. Results from meal photos, nutrition label photos, typed descriptions and barcode lookups can be wrong: an AI can misjudge a food or a portion size, misread a label, or miss an ingredient, and Open Food Facts is a public database whose entries come from volunteers and may be incomplete or out of date. Check the numbers, and edit an entry before you rely on it. Do not use the app to manage allergies, a medical diet or a condition such as diabetes.
