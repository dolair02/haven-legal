Privacy Policy

PawRx: Pet Meds & Care (Android) · Effective October 19, 2026

PawRx: Pet Meds & Care ("the app", "we") helps you keep track of your pets' medicines, doses, vaccines, vet visits, weights, costs and notes. It is published by Dolair. This policy covers the Android app and explains what it stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have. PawRx for iPhone is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. The Android app has no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your data.
  • The app has no ads and no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of ours. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it.
  • PawRx Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Dose reminders are notifications that your phone creates by itself. They are not sent from a server.
  • You can erase everything the app holds at any time, and uninstalling the app removes it too.

What the app stores on your device

As you use the app, it saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your pets' profiles: name, species, breed, age, weight, allergies, favorite treat, notes and an optional photo.
  • Medicines and their schedules: name, dose, form, times, instructions, supply and refill details, prescribing vet, prescription number and cost.
  • Dose records: whether each dose was given or skipped, when, any note or side effect, and the name of the person who gave it.
  • Weight and mood entries, vaccine records, vet appointments, photo-journal entries (with optional photos) and care costs.
  • Household members, caregivers and handover notes, including names and phone numbers that you type in, and your own name and phone number if you add them.
  • Vet and emergency vet contacts that you enter.
  • Your settings (such as light or dark theme and whether you finished the welcome screens) and your subscription status, which the app reads from Google Play.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Please enter other people's names and phone numbers only if they are happy for you to keep them.

How the app uses your phone's other apps and features

  • Photos. You pick a photo with Android's own picker, or take one with your camera app. The app keeps a copy inside its private storage. It does not itself ask for camera or photo-library access.
  • Reminders. If you allow notifications (Android 13 and later ask you), the app schedules dose reminders with Android's alarm system and shows them on your phone. Your medicine names and times are used on the phone for this and go nowhere else. After a restart or an update the app sets the alarms again. Android may deliver a reminder a few minutes late when the phone is saving battery.
  • Calls, maps and links. When you tap a phone number, an address search or a link, the app hands it to your phone's dialer, Maps or browser. Those apps and services work under their own privacy policies, and they only see what you tapped.
  • Saving and printing. Exports and backup files are saved where you choose with Android's file picker. Printing uses Android's print service (for example "Save as PDF"). Once a file or printout leaves the app, it is in your hands, and we have no control over it. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Collar tag. The QR code is drawn on your phone. It carries, as plain text, the details you entered for the pet and owner (pet name, species, breed, allergies, your name and phone number, and your vet contacts). Anyone who scans a printed tag can read them, so include only what you are comfortable sharing.

Subscriptions and Google Play

The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to offer the optional PawRx Premium subscription. When you start, restore or manage a subscription, the app talks to Google Play, which processes the purchase and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details.

When the app's Google Play components communicate with Google, Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and device or other identifiers, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This is not your pet or household data. We do not receive it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions: the Google Play Billing permission (to offer the subscription), notifications (to show dose reminders), receiving the "boot completed" signal (to set reminders again after a restart), and network status and internet access, which the Google Play Billing library adds so it can talk to Google Play. The app's own screens are loaded from files inside the app and cannot load anything from the internet, and your pet records never need an internet connection. The app does not ask to use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or calendar.

Security

The data you enter is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. Use a screen lock to protect your phone. Communication with Google Play is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Backups and moving to a new phone

The app turns off Android's automatic backup for its data, so your records are not copied to your Google account's device backup, and they do not move to a new phone by themselves. To keep a copy or change phones, use Export to save a backup file, and "Restore from a backup file" on the new phone. Because we hold no copy, an uninstall, a factory reset or a lost phone without a backup file means the records are gone.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete any pet, medicine, record or note one by one. To erase everything at once, open the menu (your profile), find "Your data" and choose "Delete all my data". Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the app stores on your phone. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files and printouts you made are yours to delete.

Your choices and rights

You control your records entirely. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Menu, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions). Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights over personal information held about you. Since we hold none of your pet or household data, such requests mostly concern your contact with us; email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is for pet owners and caregivers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
