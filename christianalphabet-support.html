Frequently Asked Questions

🔊 Why is there no sound playing on my iPad or iPhone?
Please check the following quick tips:
• Check that your physical mute switch (or Control Center Silent Mode icon) is not turned on.
• Increase your device volume using the volume up button.
• In the app, tap the Play (▶️) button on the audio narrator bar at the top of the page.
📅 How does the 3-day free trial work?
Every family receives a full 3-day free trial with unlimited access to all 26 letters, voice narrations, and bedtime prayers. There is zero charge today. If you choose to continue after 3 days, your subscription will renew automatically ($5.99/month or $49.99/year). You can cancel anytime before the trial ends without being charged.
❌ How do I manage or cancel my Apple App Store subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly and securely through your Apple ID:
1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select God's Wonderful Alphabet to change or cancel your subscription.
🔄 How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open God's Wonderful Alphabet on your new iPad or iPhone, tap Unlock Full Book or Account, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your previous access will be restored immediately at zero cost.
