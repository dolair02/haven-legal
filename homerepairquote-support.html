Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3-day free trial work?
When you download Home Repair Quote, you receive a full 3-day free trial granting unrestricted access to all features (AI quote decryption, blind bidding, and license checks). After 3 days, you can choose to continue with Monthly ($7.99/mo) or Annual ($69.99/yr) Pro access.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed securely through your Apple ID:
1. Open your iPhone or iPad Settings.
2. Tap your Apple ID / Name at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Home Repair Quote to modify or cancel your plan.
How do I restore my purchases on a new device?
Open Home Repair Quote, tap the StoreKit Paywall or Settings banner, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your entitlements will restore instantly through Apple StoreKit 2.
