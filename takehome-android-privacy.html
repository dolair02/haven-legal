Privacy Policy

Takehome: Job Profit Tracker (Android) · Effective October 21, 2026

Takehome: Job Profit Tracker ("the app", "we") helps contractors and tradespeople see what each job actually earned after costs. It is published by Dolair. This policy covers the Android app and explains what it stores, what it sends off your device, and the choices you have. Takehome for iPhone is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • Your jobs, clients, notes, costs, time entries and photos are stored on your phone only. The app has no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your data.
  • The app has no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools of ours. We do not sell personal information.
  • Takehome Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Google Play components used by the app may collect limited device and technical information, described below.
  • You can delete any job at any time, and deleting the app removes everything it stores on your phone.

What the app stores on your device

As you use the app, it saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Jobs you create: title, client name and phone number, job address, gate code and notes, date, status, quoted price (including quote line items and optional add-ons), and whether it is paid.
  • Costs you log against each job, such as a description, a category and an amount.
  • Time you track: clock-in and clock-out times, your hourly labor cost, and the labor cost added to a job.
  • Photos you attach to a job. You pick each photo with Android's own file picker, and the app keeps a resized copy inside its private storage. The original stays where it was.
  • Your setup choices and your Pro plan status.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Android's backup feature, if it is turned on for your device, may include this app's data in your device backup in your Google account. That backup is made and controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it.

Texting and calling clients

When you tap On My Way, Share Quote or Call, the app hands the job over to your phone's own apps. Call opens the phone app with the client's number. On My Way and Share Quote open your messaging app (or your phone's share sheet) with the message already filled in, and the client's number if you saved one. Nothing is sent until you press Send in that app, and the message goes through that app and your carrier, not through us. The app does not read your contacts, text messages or call history, and we do not receive the number or the message.

Subscriptions and Google Play

The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to offer the optional Takehome Pro subscription. When you start, restore or manage a subscription, the app talks to Google Play, which processes the purchase and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details.

When the app's Google Play components communicate with Google, Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and device or other identifiers, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This is not your job data. We do not receive it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

The app asks for internet access and network status so that Google Play Billing can reach Google Play and so links open, and for the Google Play Billing permission. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or calendar.

Security

The data you enter is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. Use a screen lock to protect your phone. Communication with Google Play is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. To delete a job, open it and choose Delete Job; this removes its costs and photos too. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the app stores on your phone. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your choices and rights

You control your records entirely, and you can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Menu, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions). Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights over personal information held about you. Since we hold none of your job data, such requests mostly concern your contact with us; email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for adults who run jobs and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app where required.
