Frequently Asked Questions

How does Haven protect my financial data?
Haven operates on a 100% on-device architecture. When you set a master password, all holdings, transactions, and rules are encrypted using 256-bit AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation. Your data never touches any external cloud server or third-party database.
How do I manage, cancel, or change my subscription?
Haven subscriptions ($4.99/mo or $49.99/yr with a 3-day free trial) are managed directly through Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time:
1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your Apple ID / Name at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Haven: Private Wealth & Risk to cancel or change your plan.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Inside the Haven app, open the Settings tab, scroll down to the Subscription section, and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID used to make the initial purchase, full access will be unlocked instantly.
How do I import my bank statements?
Navigate to the Statement Cleaner tab. You can drag and drop any CSV or TSV statement exported from your bank, or copy-paste text directly. Haven automatically parses the columns, scrubs noisy bank transaction descriptions into clean merchant names, detects predatory fees, and tracks recurring subscriptions.
How do I export my data to Excel, Word, or PDF?
Tap the Export button located in the top header or within the Settings tab. You can choose:
• CSV Spreadsheet: Formatted for Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, and Google Sheets.
• Word Document (.doc): Clean executive summary with tables and allocation breakdowns.
• PDF File: Print-ready report with complete portfolio analytics.
• Encrypted .haven Vault: Full backup file with password protection for secure transfer between devices.
How do I request a refund?
Because all billing is handled directly by Apple Media Services, refund requests are processed according to Apple's standard policies. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find your Haven subscription, and select "Request a refund".
