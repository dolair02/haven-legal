Delete your account and data

Overload Workout Log · Published by Dolair

You can delete your Overload account, and the data stored with it, in either of two ways.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Overload Workout Log and tap the Account tab.
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap "Delete my account", then "Yes, delete everything".

Your account and synced data are erased right away.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot open the app, or the in-app deletion does not work, email dolair02@gmail.com from the address you registered with, using the subject "Delete my Overload account". We will confirm by reply and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, user ID, and password hash.
  • Your synced data: your workouts and your exercise list.

What is not affected

  • Data stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.
  • Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

If you never created an account, we hold no personal data about you and there is nothing to delete.
