❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the $5 Anti-Flake Escrow work?

Both buyer and seller place a refundable $5 deposit into escrow when confirming an in-person exchange. When both parties meet at the agreed Safe Spot and scan the check-in code, both deposits are immediately returned in full ($0 fee). If one party cancels without notice or fails to show up within 15 minutes, their $5 deposit is automatically forfeited and paid directly to the attending party as compensation.

How do I cancel or manage my Handoff Premium subscription?

All subscriptions (including the 3-day free trial) are managed directly by Apple. To cancel before renewal, open your iPhone's Settings > tap your Name / Apple ID > tap Subscriptions > select Handoff > tap Cancel Subscription.

What should I do if a buyer/seller ghosts me at a meetup?

In the app's live deal tracker, tap "Report Flake / No-Show" after the 15-minute grace window. The GPS radar will verify that you arrived within the Safe Spot geofence and that the other party failed to arrive. The escrow system will release the $5 penalty payout directly to your in-app wallet balance, ready for instant debit cashout.

How do I request an account or data deletion?

You can delete your account and all associated telemetry at any time in the app profile under Settings > Privacy > Delete Account, or by emailing support@handoff.trade with your registered email address.

🚨 In Case of Physical Emergency

If you ever feel unsafe or experience a crime during an exchange, leave immediately and call 911 (or your local emergency number). Handoff will cooperate fully with local law enforcement by providing timestamped transaction and Safe Spot telemetry upon official request.
